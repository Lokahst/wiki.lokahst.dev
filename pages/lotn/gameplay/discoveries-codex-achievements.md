## Discoveries

LotN records newly visited biomes and dimensions, along with exploration milestones from the config.

| Discovery | Default overall XP |
|---|---|
| New biome | 0.5 |
| New dimension | 3.0 |
| Exploration milestone | 1.5 |

The default milestones are reaching Y `180` and descending below Y `-20`.

## Codex

The `/lotn codex` menu shows a Character Record and four statistics pages:

| Page | Recorded statistics |
|---|---|
| Character Record | Character name, overall level, Legacy rank, playtime, difficulty, Legendary skills, and completion |
| World | Biomes and dimensions visited, and villages discovered |
| Combat | Mobs and bosses defeated, damage dealt/received/blocked, and deaths |
| Fishing | Total and unique catches, highest rarity, treasure, and most valuable catch |
| Survival | Frostbite, broken legs, injuries treated, dangerous-cold time, and Fast Travels |

Achievements are shown separately in the Quest Journal's Achievements category.
